# Code of Conduct

Contributors and maintainers are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate of differing experience levels. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers at **keelmatrix@gmail.com** with the relevant repository, dates, links, participants, and any safe supporting context. Do not use the vulnerability-reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for conduct matters, and do not include credentials or sensitive cassette material.

Maintainers will acknowledge reports, protect reporter privacy where practical, investigate fairly, and take proportionate action. Possible actions include a request to stop, moderation or removal of content, temporary restrictions, or permanent bans. Reports involving an immediate threat should also be reported to the relevant local authorities or platform safety channel.
